Research projects

All projects →

Magnetic wall-climbing welding robot for pumped-storage pipes

A magnetic mobile platform, manipulator and vision sensors support research on seam perception, path planning and welding control inside pressure pipes.

Intelligent robotic welding of saddle-shaped joints

3D scanning, groove reconstruction and path planning support robotic multipass welding experiments on complex intersecting joints.

Rail-guided intelligent welding robot

A magnetic rail, laser seam tracking and weld-pool monitoring support multipass planning, closed-loop process control and on-site welding trials.

About the laboratory

Founded by Professor Shanben Chen in March 2000 and currently directed by him, the laboratory at Shanghai Jiao Tong University studies sensing, decision-making and intelligent execution for robotic welding. Its work connects environmental understanding with adaptive process control.
