Vision-guided adaptive robotic grinding

Visual sensing and adaptive machining

Variations in curved welds and service-deformed components make fixed taught paths difficult to reuse. This research starts from measured geometry, generates machining trajectories and uses observations after machining to guide subsequent actions.

For spatial weld beads, local point-cloud features identify the region of interest. Key-point extraction and continuous curve fitting generate the grinding path, while robot manipulability and pose variation guide joint-configuration optimization.

For turbine-seal fine grinding, 3D reconstruction supports coarse-path generation. Image-texture analysis identifies residual material and estimates orientation corrections, while force sensing supports force-position control. These methods have been studied in separate curved-weld grinding and turbine-seal remanufacturing experiments.
