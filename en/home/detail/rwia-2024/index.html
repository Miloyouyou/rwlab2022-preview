RWIA 2024 / 15th Chinese Conference on Robotic Welding in Shanghai

Academic exchange

2024-12-18

Academic presentations, technology exchange and a technical visit addressed robotic welding, intelligence and automation. Professor Shanben Chen chaired the conference; the official report records over 200 delegates.

Dates: 2024-11-15 to 2024-11-17. Location: Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai Buckingham Palace Hotel.

The event dates and the report publication date are recorded separately.
