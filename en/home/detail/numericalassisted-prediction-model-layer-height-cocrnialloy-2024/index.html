Numerical-assisted prediction model of layer height for Co-Cr-Ni-alloy direct energy deposition

2024 Additive Manufacturing, 83:104060

Xu Li; Kanghong Zhu; Huabin Chen

Research question

How does melt-pool overflow change layer height during multilayer laser deposition, and how can it support prediction?

Method

The work combines force analysis, three-dimensional flow simulation and temperature/image observations to examine melt-pool overflow. Simulation outputs and experimental features feed an RF-LSTM model for layer-height prediction.

Main findings

For the Co-Cr-Ni deposition data evaluated in the paper, the numerical-assisted RF-LSTM model reached an R² of 0.9398 for layer-height prediction, exceeding the listed simulation-only and random-forest alternatives. The study links melt-pool flow analysis with data-driven prediction.
