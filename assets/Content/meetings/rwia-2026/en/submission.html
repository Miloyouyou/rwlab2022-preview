RWIA & CCRW 2026

Call for Papers

Contributions are invited on intelligent robotic welding and related manufacturing technologies. Abstract submission closes on November 30, 2026.

Topics

  1. S01Intelligent robotic welding and manufacturing systems
  2. S02Special-purpose welding robots and embodied AI
  3. S03Autonomous planning and decision-making for robotic welding
  4. S04Multimodal sensing and process-state estimation in welding
  5. S05Weld-quality monitoring, assessment, and feedback control
  6. S06Foundation models and knowledge-driven methods for welding
  7. S07Digital twins, process modeling, simulation, and optimization
  8. S08Multi-robot coordination and welding in challenging environments
  9. S09Intelligent, digital, and flexible welding equipment
  10. S10Related topics in intelligent manufacturing

Manuscript preparation and submission

  1. Write in English using the TIWM SPLNPROC Word template and follow the series formatting instructions.
  2. Maximum lengths: Feature Article, 20 pages; Regular Paper, 12 pages; Technical Notes, 6 pages.
  3. Submit via the TIWM online system and email a copy to the conference secretariat.
  4. An English manuscript based on a CCRW submission must comply with originality, prior-publication, overlap and copyright requirements. Disclose related submissions or publications to the editors. Contact the secretariat for translation guidance.

Review and publication

English-language papers must pass peer review to enter the TIWM publication plan. Publication is planned in 2027; acceptance and volume allocation are editorial decisions. Registration payment does not guarantee acceptance.

Selected papers are planned for Transactions on Intelligent Welding Manufacturing (TIWM), a Springer Nature book series. Approximately 15 papers are expected per planned volume. Submissions may be made between the full-paper deadlines; placement depends on peer review and the editorial schedule.
