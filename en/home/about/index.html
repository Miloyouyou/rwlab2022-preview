About the Laboratory

Intelligent robotic welding · Founded in March 2000

Founded by Professor Shanben Chen in March 2000 and currently directed by him, the laboratory at Shanghai Jiao Tong University studies sensing, decision-making and intelligent execution for robotic welding. Its work connects environmental understanding with adaptive process control.

Building on research in visual sensing, dynamic welding-process control, knowledge modeling and robotic systems, the core faculty investigate 3D seam reconstruction, multi-layer multi-pass planning, multimodal process monitoring and data-physics modeling. Recent studies also address stereo vision, semantic-geometric reasoning and embodied pre-weld perception.

Research is evaluated on robotic platforms and representative components, including complex saddle-shaped joints and pipelines, and supported by collaboration, academic exchange and graduate education. Reported capabilities and performance remain tied to their published validation conditions.

Earlier work includes locally autonomous intelligent welding robots (LAIWR), flexible welding cells and coordinated multi-robot systems. Recent research builds on this foundation with 3D vision, welding knowledge and data-driven methods for complex manufacturing tasks.

Shanben Chen
