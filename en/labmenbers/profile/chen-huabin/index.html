Huabin Chen

Research Professor, Doctoral Supervisor

Research interests

Intelligent robotic welding / Intelligent control of welding dynamics / Intelligent thermal manufacturing

hbchen@sjtu.edu.cn

Huabin Chen studies intelligent welding in complex environments, including multimodal perception, quality monitoring, point-cloud-based path planning and intelligent thermal manufacturing. His work combines process knowledge, data modelling and system integration for welding and additive manufacturing of large-scale equipment.

Associated research

  • Analytical model for weld penetration prediction in finite plates with time-varying heat sources →
  • LLM-enhanced stereo vision perception and TCP pose generation for embodied AI-enabled robotic welding →
  • A Novel Point Cloud-Driven Framework for Enhanced Multi-Views Model Reconstruction and Robotic Arc Welding Trajectory Generation →
  • A novel robotic multi-layer multi-pass welding adaptive path generation method based on point cloud slicing and Transformer for saddle-shaped weld seams →
  • Robotic MAG welding defects and quality assessment with a defect threshold decision model-driven method →
  • Real-time prediction of temperature field during welding by data-mechanism driving →
  • A novel filling strategy for robotic multi-layer and multi-pass welding based on point clouds for saddle-shaped weld seams →
  • Online monitoring and penetration recognition in all-position TIG welding of nuclear power pipeline →
  • Key information perception and control strategies for intelligent welding in complex scenes →
  • Multimodal Sensing for Online Weld-defect Monitoring and Process Optimization →
  • Adaptive intelligent-welding software for pumped-storage pressure pipes →
