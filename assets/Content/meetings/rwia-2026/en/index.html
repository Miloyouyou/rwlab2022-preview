The 9th RWIA and the 16th CCRW will take place in Shanghai on December 11–13, 2026. Hosted by Shanghai Jiao Tong University, the joint conference brings together researchers and practitioners in robotic welding, intelligent robots and advanced manufacturing.

Conference theme

AI-enabled robotic welding: from intelligent sensing to autonomous manufacturing

The RWIA series began in Shanghai in 2002. This edition addresses joint fit-up variation, challenging environments, process uncertainty and welding distortion, with a focus on multimodal sensing, foundation models, embodied AI, digital twins, and physics-informed and data-driven methods. Discussions will connect sensing, planning and adaptive control with practical welding applications.
