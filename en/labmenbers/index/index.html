FOUNDER & DIRECTOR

Shanben Chen

Founder and Director | Professor, Doctoral Supervisor

Shanben Chen founded the laboratory in March 2000 and continues to lead its research in intelligent robotic welding. His work spans visual and multisensor perception, modelling and intelligent control of welding dynamics, locally autonomous welding robots and multi-robot manufacturing systems.
