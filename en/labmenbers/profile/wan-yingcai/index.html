Yingcai Wan

Postdoctoral Researcher

Research interests

Intelligent welding robots / 3D vision and scene reconstruction / Semantic–geometric perception / Embodied intelligence and LLM-driven autonomy / Pose and trajectory planning

Yingcai Wan is a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Robotics, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research focuses on autonomous robotic operation in complex environments, including 3D scene reconstruction, welding-target perception and geometric modelling. He investigates task understanding, TCP pose generation and trajectory planning by integrating large language models, geometric constraints and process knowledge.

He has participated in national key programmes, Shanghai science and technology projects and industrial R&D. His work spans intelligent welding, humanoid robots, dexterous manipulation and exoskeleton robots, with research and engineering experience in robotic perception, intelligent decision-making and system integration.
