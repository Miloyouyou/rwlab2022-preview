Research platforms

Connecting visual sensing, robotic execution and experimental validation.

Mobile Intelligent Welding Robot ↗

A mobile platform for studying visual perception, task planning and coordinated mobile-manipulator operation in constrained welding spaces.

Robotic Welding Platform for Intersecting Joints ↗

3D scanning, groove reconstruction, planning and robot experiments support validation of multi-pass welding for saddle-shaped joints.

Multisensor Welding-process Monitoring ↗

Weld-pool imaging, arc acoustics and electrical measurements support process-state recognition and welding-quality research.
