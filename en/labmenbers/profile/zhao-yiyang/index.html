Yiyang Zhao

Postdoctoral Researcher

804311109@sjtu.edu.cn

Biography

Yiyang Zhao is a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Welding and Laser Manufacturing, School of Materials Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where he obtained his doctorate. His research focuses on wall-climbing welding robots and intelligent monitoring of welding processes.

He has led three projects, including an NSFC Young Scientists Fund project and industry-sponsored research, participated in national major projects and national and provincial key R&D programmes, and published more than 20 SCI-indexed papers.

Research interests

  • Robot motion control
  • Weld formation quality
  • Intelligent welding equipment
