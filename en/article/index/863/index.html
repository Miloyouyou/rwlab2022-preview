Call for papers 2013 3rd International Conference on Advanced Materials and Information Technology Processing (AMITP 2013)

Updated：2013.07.01 Historical record

Call for papers
2013 3rd International Conference on Advanced Materials and Information Technology Processing (AMITP 2013)
http://www.hkimss.com/amitp2013/
October 1-2, 2013, Los Angeles, CA, USA
Honorable Professors,
We are very sincere to invite you to take part in 2013 3rd International Conference on Advanced Materials and Information Technology Processing (AMITP 2013) in October 1-2, 2013, Los Angeles, CA, USA. AMITP 2013 provides an opportunity for prominent researchers, engineers and practitioners to present the latest research results, innovations, and potential future directions in Advanced Materials and Information Technology Processing.
Publication
All AMITP 2013 accepted papers will be published on international journal “WIT Transactions on Engineering Sciences (ISSN: 1743-3533)”, which will be indexed by Indexed by Elsevier: SCOPUS www.scopus.com and Ei Compendex (CPX) www.ei.org/.
Submission
The submission system is https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=amitp2013
Important Date
Paper submission due: July 20, 2013
Contact
amitp2013@yeah.net
[7月20日截稿 洛杉矶] 2013年第三届先进材料与信息技术处理国际学术会议(EI检索)
投稿范围：材料科学、机械制造、化学工程、信息工程、计算机科学及其相关学科。
大会接收的文章全部由WIT Transactions on Engineering Sciences (ISSN: 1743-3533)出版，并将被EI检索。
本次会议将挑选约50篇优秀论文直接发表在其他SCI期刊，具体事项另行通知。
会议网站：http://www.hkimss.com/amitp2013/
咨询电话：18502799558
联系邮件：amitp2013@yeah.net
稿件可在投稿系统或联系邮箱amitp2013@yeah.net提交，任选一种方式即可。
