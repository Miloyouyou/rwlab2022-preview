A novel robotic multi-layer multi-pass welding adaptive path generation method based on point cloud slicing and Transformer for saddle-shaped weld seams

Journal of Manufacturing Processes

2025-03-22 Journal of Manufacturing Processes · 141 · 1578-1594

Kai Liu, Zhongxi Sheng, Wei Zhang, Zhengbin Zhong, Xiao Yang, Huabin Chen, Runquan Xiao

Cylinder-axis localization establishes a slicing frame for groove reconstruction and multi-pass planning; a decoder-free Transformer predicts welding speeds from the geometric slice sequence.

The findings are specific to the methods, materials and validation conditions of the cited paper. Perception or planning accuracy is not a substitute for independent weld-quality assessment.

DOI: 10.1016/j.jmapro.2025.03.071 ↗
