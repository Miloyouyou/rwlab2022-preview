2002.09-2006.07 Shanghai Jiao Tong University, School of Materials Science and Engineering (Bachelor)

I researched on related content of initial welding position recognition and guiding in robot automation welding process in period of master. Now I am mainly engaged in research work of welding automation and robot welding intellectualization technology.
I publish two papers in the domestic and foreign academic magazines, including one SCI collection and one EI collection. I also apply two patents of invention. Now I am International magazine special reviewer of Sensor Review and Industrial Robot.

Projects involved:
1、"The Eleventh Five-year" pre-research project from the National Defense Science and Technology Committee: Robotic arc welding technology of new aluminum structures in spacecraft
2006.10-2009.10
2、Study of development of welding pool monitoring system for MAG welding for KUKA robot,2009-2010
