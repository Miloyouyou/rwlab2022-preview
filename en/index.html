01 / THE LABORATORY

About the laboratory

Founded by Professor Shanben Chen in March 2000 and currently directed by him, the laboratory at Shanghai Jiao Tong University studies sensing, decision-making and intelligent execution for robotic welding. Its work connects environmental understanding with adaptive process control.

02 / RESEARCH

Perception, planning and control.

All research areas →
01

3D Vision and Seam Reconstruction

Multi-view scanning, point-cloud registration and groove-feature extraction provide geometric input for robotic path and pose planning.

↗02

Multi-layer Multi-pass Planning

Groove geometry supports layer/pass filling, spatial path generation and welding-speed prediction for complex intersecting joints.

↗03

Multimodal Monitoring and Intelligent Control

Weld-pool images, arc sound and electrical signals inform process-state recognition, defect warnings and intelligent control research.
