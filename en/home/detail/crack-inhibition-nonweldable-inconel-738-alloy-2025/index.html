Crack inhibition of non-weldable Inconel 738 alloy in ultrasound-assisted laser directed energy deposition

2025 Materials Science and Engineering A, 921, 147607

Ziyu Tian; Yi Zheng; Haodong Wang; Shuai Wang; Junmei Chen; Huabin Chen; Chun Yu; Jijin Xu; Hao Lu

Research question

Can ultrasonic assistance and heat-input adjustment jointly improve microstructure and mechanical properties while suppressing cracking in laser-deposited Inconel 738?

Method

Ultrasonic assistance is combined with scan-speed adjustment, microstructural and fracture analysis, and room-temperature tensile testing. The study examines how grain-boundary geometry and carbide distributions affect crack initiation and propagation.

Main findings

The studied process combinations produced deposits with no observed cracks. At room temperature, specimen S-U9 reached an ultimate tensile strength of 1490±10 MPa and elongation of 17.8±0.55%, demonstrating the potential of coordinated ultrasonic and thermal processing.
