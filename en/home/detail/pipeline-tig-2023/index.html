Online monitoring and penetration recognition in all-position TIG welding of nuclear power pipeline

Journal of Manufacturing Processes

2023-12-02 Journal of Manufacturing Processes · 108 · 889-902

Weihua Liu, Minhua Chen, Kanghong Zhu, Liangrui Wang, Huabin Chen, Hao Lu

An internal crawler acquires groove, back-side weld pool and solidified-bead features for all-position TIG welding; a random-forest model combines these features with process parameters to identify penetration states.

The findings are specific to the methods, materials and validation conditions of the cited paper. Perception or planning accuracy is not a substitute for independent weld-quality assessment.

DOI: 10.1016/j.jmapro.2023.11.035 ↗
