Na Lv

更新时间：2014.03.29 历史资料

Na Lv postdoctor

Telephone：15201858060
Affiliation：Building F.203, School of Materials Science and   Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai 200240, China
·Dr Na Lv obtained her PhD degree in Materials Process Engineering, specialized in robotic welding automation at the Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2014. In recent years, she has been doing research in the areas of the intelligent information acquisition and processing of welding process, robot intelligent system applications and robot welding automation. She has already published 16 papers, and there are 9 papers has published in international journal such as Industrial Robot, Journal of Materials Processing Technology, Sensor Review and International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology, et al. As an backbone of scientific research, she has finish three National Natural Science Funds project, three important research project of Shanghai CST and be assistant to handle the RWIA ‘2010 international intelligent robotic welding conference, etc.
