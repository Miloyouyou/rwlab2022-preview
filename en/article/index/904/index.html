Shanchun Wei

Updated：2014.03.28 Historical record

Shanchun Wei lecturer/Doctor
Email: scwei@sjtu.edu.cn
Tel:   021-34202740-803（China）
Affiliation: Room 203, Building F, School of Material science and engineering
Shanghai Jiao Tong University, No.800 Dongchuan Road, Shanghai, 200240, China
Dr. Shanchun Wei obtained his PhD degree in Materials Process Engineering, specialized in robotic welding automation at the Shanghai Jiao Tong University in Sep，2013. His main research direction is the design and manufacturing of vision sensors, the configuration of visual system and the calibration of camera. He also engaged in robot welding process identification and guidance, and other related intelligent robot technology, etc. His mentor is Professor Shanben Chen.
Education
2009-2013   Doctor, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, School of Materials Science and Engineering,
2006-2009   Master, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, School of Materials Science and Engineering
2002-2006   Bachelor, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, School of Materials Science and Engineering
 Research Area:
 Sensing, Welding process automation, Robotics and automation welding, Image processing
